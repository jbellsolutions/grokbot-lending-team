# Open questions

The bot asks the owner the setup questions itself (`skills/getting-started`). The questions below are decisions to make around it.

## Before setup

1. **Separate Grok account?** GrokBot gives each user account one shared computer, so every bot in that account can see the same files, browser sessions and logins. If the owner already uses GrokBot for another business, the cleanest setup is a separate Grok account for the lending team. The other option is to use the same account and rely on the isolation guard in every routine.
2. **Plan size.** Grok Bot needs Premium or higher. On Premium, run phase 1 only, then watch **Weekly usage** for a week before adding phase 2. Super and Ultra have larger weekly allowances.
3. **GoHighLevel access.** Can the GHL account create a Private Integration and a new user seat? If the account sits under an agency that blocks either, who at the agency can allow it?
4. **Referral fees and state rules.** Which partner types and states can legally be paid a referral fee for business-purpose loans? Confirm with counsel before any outreach mentions a partner program with pay.

## Phase 1

5. **Which form is live?** The GHL Operator's audit answers this, but the owner confirms which one the website's partner page should use.
6. **Hours.** Should routines run Monday to Friday only (the default), or Saturday too?
7. **Assistant.** Does the assistant own partner replies and call tasks, or only call tasks?
8. **Program facts.** What may the team say about speed, requirements and minimums? This becomes `program-facts.md`.
9. **Text messaging.** Is A2P approved? Until it is, every touch is email only.
10. **Group intro call.** Monthly? Which day and time?
11. **Instant partner replies.** Partner replies are read each morning and at midday. Should the GHL Operator add a webhook so they reach the team at once? See the optional step at the end of START-HERE.

## Phase 2

12. **Smartlead or Instantly?** Smartlead is recommended. Whichever one is picked, the account should be the owner's own, and someone needs to set up the cold domains and inboxes.
13. **Existing partner campaigns.** Should any partner campaign already under way move into the owner's own cold email tool, starting from the next unsent step?
14. **Reactivation pace.** How many old contacts a day?
15. **Standing approvals.** Should interested outbound replies get the booking link automatically, or wait for a yes each time?

## Later, each a separate decision

16. **Intent Scout.** Social intent signals for borrower and partner leads (the reserved agent).
17. **Partner newsletter, authority content and a full KPI dashboard.**
18. **Loan-processing helper.** Out of scope here on purpose. Any agent past Quick App Sent needs its own design, with a person in the loop at every step.
