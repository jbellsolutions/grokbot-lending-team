# Reserved: Intent Scout

**Status:** not built. A placeholder so the team has a known slot for it.

## The idea

Watch public social posts and forums for people who say they need what ClearBlu offers. For example, an investor asking where to find a fix and flip or DSCR lender, or a mortgage broker asking who funds investor deals they can't place. Sort them into high, medium and low intent, and hand the high-intent ones to the Borrower Desk or the Partner Recruiter as warm leads. Owner-occupied home buyers are not ClearBlu's audience here and are never flagged.

## Why it's reserved

- It depends on a separate decision about which signal service to use and how it's paid for. See [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
- It adds daily usage. Add it only after every other agent has run five clean weekdays in a row with room left on the weekly meter, and Larry decides to.

## Rules it would follow

- Public posts only. No scraping personal contact details.
- First contact only where the platform allows it, and in a reply Larry approved.
- The same compliance rules as every other agent.
