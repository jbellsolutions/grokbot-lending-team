# Reserved: Intent Scout

**Status:** not built. A placeholder so the team has a known slot for it.

## The idea

Watch public social posts and forums for people who say they need what the business offers. For example, an investor asking where to find a fix-and-flip lender, or a realtor asking who funds deals fast. Sort them into high, medium and low intent, and hand the high-intent ones to the Borrower Desk or the Partner Recruiter as warm leads.

## Why it's reserved

- It depends on a separate decision about which signal service to use and how it's paid for. See [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).
- It adds daily usage. Add it only after phase 2 has run cleanly for a few weeks with room left on the weekly meter.

## Rules it would follow

- Public posts only. No scraping personal contact details.
- First contact only where the platform allows it, and in a reply the owner approved.
- The same compliance rules as every other agent.
