# Lending compliance check

Run every draft through this list. Fix what you can and flag what you can't.

1. **No promises.** Remove any promise or hint of approval, rate, term, amount, leverage, closing time, a term sheet, or partner income. Replace it with the quick app (about 30 seconds) or "Larry will walk you through options on a quick call".
2. **Approved facts only.** Every claim about a program must appear in `context/program-facts.md` under "May say". Remove anything listed under "Never say".
3. **No fees.** No referral fee, commission, split or partner pay, not even a range. Larry covers terms on the intro call.
4. **Investor loans only.** If the reader seems to want a loan on a home they'll live in, don't draft. Route it to Larry. Never mention ClearBlu Home Loans or clearbluhomeloans.com.
5. **Fair lending.** No mention of, or targeting by, any protected trait or neighborhood.
6. **Email footer.** Postal address and an unsubscribe link are present.
7. **Text rules.** A2P approved, the person consented, the send time is 9 am to 8 pm in their local time.
8. **No AI mention, no false identity.** If the reader asked whether this is automated, don't draft. Route it to a person.
9. **No private data.** No financial details, ID numbers or documents in the message.
10. **Name and wording.** The company is "ClearBlu Capital Group", never "Clear Blue". Where a message says what ClearBlu does, it uses Larry's sentence from program-facts.md word for word, not the old "We fund business-purpose real estate loans..." line.
11. **Links.** Only the links in program-facts.md. A borrower message offers the quick app first.
12. **Tone.** Short, warm, one ask, in Larry's voice.

Result: write "compliance: pass" or "compliance: flagged: [reason]" on the draft's line in the daily note.
