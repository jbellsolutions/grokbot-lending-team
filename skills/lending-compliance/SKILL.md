# Lending compliance check

Run every draft through this list. Fix what you can and flag what you can't.

1. **No promises.** Remove any promise or hint of approval, rate, term, amount, closing time or partner income. Replace it with "happy to walk you through options on a quick call".
2. **Approved facts only.** Every claim about a program must appear in `context/program-facts.md` under "May say". Remove anything listed under "Never say".
3. **No fees.** No referral fee, commission or split amounts. Fee questions go to the owner.
4. **Business purpose.** If the reader seems to want a loan on a home they'll live in, route to the owner.
5. **Fair lending.** No mention of, or targeting by, any protected trait or neighborhood.
6. **Email footer.** Postal address and an unsubscribe link are present.
7. **Text rules.** A2P approved, the person consented, the send time is 9 am to 8 pm in their local time.
8. **No AI mention, no false identity.** If the reader asked whether this is automated, don't draft. Route it to a person.
9. **No private data.** No financial details, ID numbers or documents in the message.
10. **Tone.** Short, warm, one ask, in the owner's voice.

Result: write "compliance: pass" or "compliance: flagged: [reason]" on the draft's line in the daily note.
